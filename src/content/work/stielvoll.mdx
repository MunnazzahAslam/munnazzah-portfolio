<Brief>
A small food brand needs a shop that feels like the brand, not a template. Stielvoll had to make the product itself the experience, and still take real orders: pickup or same-day delivery in a two-hour slot, paid online, in German and English.
</Brief>

<Features>
  <Feature title="A jelly you can pull" image="/work/stielvoll/feature-1.jpg" alt="A hand dragging an orange popsicle that bends like jelly">
    A real-time 3D popsicle that bends where you pull it and wobbles back into shape.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Bites, cut per pixel" image="/work/stielvoll/feature-2.jpg" alt="A pink popsicle with a bite taken out of the top">
    Tap it and you take a bite. The bite is cut in a custom shader, tooth marks and all.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="A box of six, priced live" image="/work/stielvoll/feature-3.jpg" alt="The box builder with a counter for each flavour and the box price">
    Mix any six or twelve flavours and see the price and the saving as you go.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Slots and Stripe Checkout" image="/work/stielvoll/feature-4.jpg" alt="Stripe's payment page on a phone">
    Pickup or delivery in a two-hour slot, paid through Stripe Checkout in test mode, then an order number and a keep-frozen tip.
  </Feature>
</Features>

<Decisions>
  <Decision n="01" title="Bites in the shader, not the mesh" tradeoff="A bite is a cut in the surface, not new geometry, so there are no crumbs.">
    The popsicle keeps one simple mesh; each bite is a circle the fragment shader discards against. The jelly simulation never has to rebuild geometry, so it stays cheap enough for a phone.
  </Decision>
  <Decision n="02" title="A spring lattice, not a physics engine" tradeoff="Only the popsicle behaves physically; anything more would need the library after all.">
    The jelly is a small hand-written soft-body simulation, a lattice of springs, so the shop doesn't ship a physics library for one object.
  </Decision>
  <Decision n="03" title="Prices only on the server" tradeoff="Checkout waits for one server round trip before Stripe opens.">
    The browser only says what's in the cart. The server prices it from the shop's own data, checks the slot still has room, and creates the Stripe session.
  </Decision>
</Decisions>

<Engineering stack={["Next.js", "React Three Fiber", "Stripe", "Supabase", "next-intl"]} flow={["Cart in the browser", "Server prices it", "Stripe Checkout", "Webhook marks it paid", "Orders admin"]}>
Each slot takes six orders and is recounted after every order, so two buyers can't overfill it. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see a still popsicle instead of the jelly. Lighthouse scores are measured on the live build at launch.
</Engineering>
