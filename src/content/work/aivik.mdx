<Brief>
A software and AI studio needed a site that explains five services quickly, in English and German, and turns interest into a conversation. Its assistant had to answer like a person at the studio would: briefly, without inventing prices or timelines, and without inventing leads.
</Brief>

<Features>
  <Feature title="An animated hero" image="/work/aivik/feature-1.jpg" alt="The hero, 'Build. Scale. Automate.', over a field of moving particles">
    A live particle field behind three words, with the offer and two ways in right beside it.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Service cards that open" image="/work/aivik/feature-2.jpg" alt="Five service cards, one opened to show what it covers">
    Five services as small live artifacts; each card opens on hover to show what it covers.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="A process drawn as you scroll" image="/work/aivik/feature-3.jpg" alt="The process timeline, its line drawing in as the page scrolls">
    The four steps of an engagement, joined by a line that draws itself as the visitor scrolls.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Mara, the assistant" image="/work/aivik/feature-4.jpg" alt="Mara's chat open over the hero, with suggested questions">
    Answers in one or two sentences, in English or German, and passes a qualified lead to the team.
  </Feature>
</Features>

<Decisions>
  <Decision n="01" title="A lead needs an email the visitor typed" tradeoff="A visitor who only leaves a phone number isn't saved as a lead.">
    The model can call a tool to save a lead, and in testing it once saved "Visitor" with the email "pending". The server now saves a lead only if the visitor typed a valid email in the conversation; otherwise the model is told nothing was saved and asks for it.
  </Decision>
  <Decision n="02" title="One pipeline for every inquiry" tradeoff="Chat leads carry less structure than the form, such as no company name.">
    Leads from the assistant and from the contact form land in the same table, the same spreadsheet and the same email, so the team checks one place.
  </Decision>
  <Decision n="03" title="German without moving English" tradeoff="German addresses that differ from the English ones need rewrites.">
    German was added later. English stays at the root and German lives under /de, so every English link already shared kept working.
  </Decision>
</Decisions>
